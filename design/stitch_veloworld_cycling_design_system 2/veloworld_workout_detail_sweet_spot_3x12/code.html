Workout Objective

Over-unders represent the benchmark physiological stimulus for lifting your Functional Threshold Power. By driving wattage 10% above FTP (270W), muscle fibers accumulate high blood lactate concentrations.

Instead of dropping into a resting valley, the interval returns to sweet spot (220W). Under this persistent moderate load, your mitochondrial enzymes are forced to metabolize and clear lactate as fuel, dramatically expanding your capacity to absorb surges during fast group rides and crits.

Coach Pacing Notes

Pro Protocol
1
Lock cadence during under valleys Keep cadence strictly between 90–95 RPM during the 2-minute under valleys to facilitate microvascular venous return without loading joints.
2
Kick or stand on 1-min surges Stand up or kick cadence to 100+ RPM on the 1-minute over peaks to recruit fast-twitch fibers, relieving saddle pressure and shocking the cardiovascular system.
3
Thermal control is mandatory Ensure smart trainer fan is on high; core body temperature will spike abruptly during Set 2. Sip isotonic fluids every 8 minutes.
